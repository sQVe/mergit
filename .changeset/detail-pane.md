---
'mergit': minor
---

Mergit shows a detail pane beside the list when the terminal is 90 columns wide or more. The pane
shows the selected thread: its repository, type, number, title, reason, age, and unread state. For a
group or repo it shows the thread counts by type and by reason. Below 90 columns the list fills the
screen, and Enter on a thread opens its detail at full width. Esc returns to the list on the same
row.
