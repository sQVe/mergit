Press Alt+j or Alt+k to move the pane you are not using. In the inbox, they scroll the preview
without moving the selection. In the focused reader, they switch to the next or previous
notification, skip group rows and folded threads, and stop at the ends. Ctrl+n and Ctrl+p do the
same in terminals that do not send Alt.

Config keys accept Alt as `<A-j>` or `<M-j>`. Remap the pair with `moveOtherPaneDown` and
`moveOtherPaneUp`.
