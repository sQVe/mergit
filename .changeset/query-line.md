Press `/` to open a search line under the list and narrow it as you type. A thread shows when its
title or repository contains every word you typed. The line shows the match count. `Enter` keeps the
text, `Esc` brings back the previous text, `Ctrl-U` clears the line, and `Backspace` on an empty
line closes it. `Esc` in the list clears an active search.
