'mergit': minor

Mergit shows the notification list as a tree you can fold. Groups and repos show a count that stays
when they are folded. Press `s` to cycle the grouping: by reason, by type, or none. The first two
group threads, then repos. With none, the list is flat: one row per thread, newest first, with the
repository in its own column. Press `z`, `space`, or `return` to fold a node, `Z` to fold all
groups, `g` and `G` to jump to the first and last row, and `J` and `K` to jump between groups. The
fold and group-jump keys do nothing in the flat list.
