Press `f` in the list to open the filter composer, a `Filter` box under the chip bar. Type a
`field:value` token, such as `author:@me`, `-repo:hups-co/platform`, `age:>3d`, or `is:pr`, or pick
one from the list with `Up`, `Down`, and `Tab`. Typing also matches field and value loosely, so
`rep plat` suggests `repo:hups-co/platform`. An empty box lists your chips and the most common
values. `Enter` adds the token as a chip and narrows the list. The list does not change while you
type. A leading `-` excludes matches. Chips show above the list. To edit a chip, move to it, press
`Tab`, change the text, and press `Enter`. `Esc` cancels the edit. `Enter` on a chip removes it.
`Esc` closes the composer, and so does `Backspace` on an empty line. `F` in the list clears all
filters.
