A focused issue now opens a tabbed reader. The header shows the repository, issue number, state,
author, title, reason, and fetch age. The Overview tab shows the issue text, and the Discussion tab
shows its comments. `h`/`l` (or `Left`/`Right`) switch tabs, and `j`/`k` and `Ctrl+D`/`Ctrl+U`
scroll the text. Each tab keeps its reading position across tab switches, return visits, and
resizes. The reader marks partial or failed loads, and shows a notice when GitHub answers as another
account. Other subjects, such as releases, show their details and a hint to open them in the
browser.
