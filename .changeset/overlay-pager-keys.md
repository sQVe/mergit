Help and messages scroll with the list's move keys. `j` and `k` (and the arrows) move one line,
Ctrl+d and Ctrl+u half a page, Ctrl+f and Ctrl+b a page, `gg` goes to the top, and `G` to the
bottom. `q` now closes them, as Esc does, and no longer quits while one is open. Ctrl+C still quits.
