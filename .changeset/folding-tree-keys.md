Zeta shows the notification list as a tree you can fold. Groups and repos show a count that stays
when they are folded. Press `s` to group by reason, repo, type, or nothing. Press `z`, `space`, or
`return` to fold a node, `Z` to fold all groups, `g` and `G` to jump to the first and last row, and
`J` and `K` to jump between groups.
